**Before this session**

- Bring an A3 drawing sheet, a 2H and HB pencil, and a 30cm scale.
- Review the standard sheet border and title block layout.
- Read through the lettering conventions below; you'll use them on every sheet this term.

Standard lettering height for titles is 5mm, for general notes 3.5mm. All lettering is upright, single-stroke, and drawn freehand — no stencils.
